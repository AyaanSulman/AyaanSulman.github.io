University and personal projects

  • ObscuraNet

    Type
    Final year project
    When
    2025
    Team
    4 students

    Private peer-to-peer messaging with no servers. Messages hop between peers through onion routing, so no single node knows both who sent a message and who received it.

    • Encryption: peers find each other on the local network and agree keys with X25519 (ECDH); sessions are encrypted with ChaCha20-Poly1305, and local files at rest with Fernet.
    • Abuse resistance: proof-of-work makes fake identities and floods expensive (Sybil and DoS resistance), and dummy traffic hides real conversations.
    • Testing: ran on 100 Docker containers, with messages delivered in under 2 seconds.
    • Explainer site: a scrollytelling walk through a message's 40-step life, with 3D visuals of proof-of-work and onion routing (Next.js, Tailwind, Framer Motion).

    Built with Python, cryptography, Docker

    ObscuraNet on GitHub Explainer site on GitHub
